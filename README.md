# Velvet Beauty Co. Retail & Marketing Performance Dashboard (2024–2025)

## Project Overview
This Tableau dashboard delivers a comprehensive analysis of Velvet Beauty Co.’s retail and marketing performance (2024–2025).
It integrates multi‑table data sources to evaluate revenue trends, campaign ROI, and marketing efficiency across channels, categories, and regions.

## Business Context
Velvet Beauty Co. is a mid‑sized cosmetics brand headquartered in New York, specialising in premium makeup and skincare.  
The dashboard supports leadership decisions by connecting sales, marketing spend, and campaign data to reveal actionable insights.

## Objectives
- Track revenue, profit, and cost of goods sold (COGS) trends across time.  
- Compare campaign performance and ROI.  
- Evaluate marketing spend efficiency by platform and region.  
- Identify high‑performing product categories and channels.

## Data Model
Four tables were connected in Tableau:
- **Sales** – transactions, revenue, profit, COGS, units sold.  
- **Channels** – sales channel metadata.
- **Marketing Spend** – campaign, platform, region, spend.  
- **Calendar** – date hierarchy and holiday flag.

Relationships were defined using primary and foreign keys to ensure integrity.

## Dashboard Features
- KPI cards for Total Revenue, Profit, COGS, and Units Sold.
- Campaign, Category, and Channel performance visuals.
- ROI comparison between marketing spend and revenue.
- Regional and platform spend matrix.
- Holiday vs non‑holiday season revenue split.  

## Key Insights
- **Summer Glow** and **Valentine’s Day** campaigns delivered the highest ROI.  
- **Skincare** and **Face** categories drove most revenue.  
- **Web Store** remains the top‑performing channel.  
- Marketing spend is highest on **Influencer** and **TikTok**, with strong returns in the East region.
